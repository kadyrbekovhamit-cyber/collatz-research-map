Rotate the structure by dragging. Zoom with the wheel or controls, select an
argument, enter its branch, and follow source/related-step links. A cut-away
slider and transparency reveal internal layers. Search includes original
Russian statements where an English label has not been curated. Open index.html
in the atlas directory; no installation, external library or idle animation.

Tao: green. Project records: blue. Other selected authors: red. Open obligations:
hollow. Drafts: separately labelled. These are authorship colors, unlike the
status colors in release1. Geometry is illustrative: it measures no mathematical
distance, proof confidence, importance, novelty or percentage solved. This is
not a protein model or an AlphaFold computation. Selected literature is not an
exhaustive history. Internal audits do not imply external peer review.

The3:27 narrated tour follows the Ingenium approach: small example, prediction,
explanation, open question. Original vector scenes and the atlas geometry were
rendered with one configured sharp/FFmpeg worker, no GPU. Voice: edge-tts,
en-US-JennyNeural, rate-3%. EN captions use actual word events; RU translations
follow corresponding English sentence/word boundaries. No audio playback or
independent ASR was performed in silent QA. Complete decode and static review
passed. Film is distributed separately from this source ZIP.

AI assisted research, drafting, checking and visualization. Authors remain
responsible for claims. No improvement over Tao, solution date or percentage
complete is claimed. Reports preserve their stated assumptions and open gaps.
